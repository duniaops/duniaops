Software Project Handover Checklist

Changing the team should not mean losing control. Use this checklist to make sure the next people can understand, build, protect and operate the software your organisation depends on.

Start the checklistProject rescue support

Review draft. This page is prepared for internal review and is not approved for public release.

View source guidance

1. Scope, ownership and decision rights

Agree what is transferring, who can make decisions and where contractual or ownership questions need separate advice.

2. Repositories and engineering history

Give the organisation administrative control and enough context to reproduce the current software.

3. Build, release and rollback

Make release steps repeatable and clarify which changes need review or approval.

4. Cloud, domains and infrastructure

Keep core accounts under the organisation's control and retain a clear record of the live environment.

5. Data, backups and recovery

Understand what the system stores and demonstrate that important data can be recovered.

6. Identities, secrets and access

Transfer access deliberately. Do not send passwords, private keys or production secrets in ordinary email or this checklist.

7. Integrations, incidents and support

Make the live service understandable before accepting ongoing operational responsibility.

8. Runbooks, people and supplier exit

Transfer the knowledge that currently lives in individuals' heads and agree when the old access ends.

9. First-week checks for the incoming team

Use evidence to choose a safe first step; do not promise a rewrite before the application is understood.

Choose the right next step

Match help to the current risk

  • Unstable or stalled: establish control and recovery priorities first.
  • Broadly stable: agree a safe support takeover and maintenance boundary.

Use with care. This checklist is an operational prompt, not legal, data-protection or security advice. Contractual ownership and personal-data responsibilities depend on the specific arrangement. For personal-data processing changes, confirm the controller/processor roles, contract and secure transfer with the responsible parties.

Guidance: NCSC guidance on using cloud platforms securely and ICO guidance on processor contracts and data sharing. The checklist turns general guidance into questions to discuss; it does not replace the organisations' own risk assessment or professional advice.

Need a safe next step?

If delivery is stalled or production is unstable, start with a recovery assessment. If the system is broadly stable, discuss a support takeover.
