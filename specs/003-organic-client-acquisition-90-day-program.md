# DuniaOps 90 Günlük Organik Müşteri Kazanım Programı

**Status:** Active — revised 4 September 2026; execution starts 7 September 2026.
**Type:** Programme
**Priority:** P1
**Period:** 7 September–5 December 2026 (90 execution days); complete-data verification 11 December 2026.
**Risk:** Medium — içerik ve sayfa çalışmaları geri döndürülebilir; izinsiz yayın, kanıtsız iddia, düşük kaliteli seri içerik veya hatalı ölçüm müşteri güvenini ve kararları bozabilir.

## Objective

UK pazarında Google üzerinden nitelikli yazılım müşterileri kazanmak için hizmet sayfaları, buyer-intent blog yazıları, kanıt varlıkları, otorite çalışması ve ölçümü tek bir yürütme sisteminde birleştirmek.

Birincil edinim odağı:

1. software project rescue ve mevcut projeyi devralma;
2. başka ekibin geliştirdiği yazılıma support ve maintenance;
3. DevOps, cloud ve Kubernetes güvenilirliği;
4. bespoke/custom software development; ve
5. AI consultancy ve production AI development.

## Delivery Model — 60 Days Production + 30 Days Optimisation

Program 60 güne kısaltılmaz. Ana üretim ilk 60 günde tamamlanır; son 30 gün yeni içerik kotası yerine indeksleme, görünürlük, dönüşüm, kanıt ve otoriteyi güçlendirir.

| Phase | Dates | Purpose | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 7–13 Sep | Measurement, ownership and approved briefs | Trusted baseline and Week 02–08 asset register |
| Core production | 14 Sep–1 Nov | Publish commercial pages, four buyer articles, first proof and checklist | Every Day-60 core asset live or has an explicit owner/date/blocker |
| Day-60 gate | 2–8 Nov; execution Day 60 is 5 Nov | Close production gaps and make the first portfolio decision | Production completeness and early-signal decision recorded |
| Optimisation | 9 Nov–5 Dec | Indexing, internal links, snippets, CRO, lead quality and authority | Evidence-led improvements completed; no quota-driven new article |
| Data verification tail | 7–11 Dec | Read delayed complete Search Console data | Day-90 ledger and next programme approved |

Publishing core assets by Week 08 gives even the latest asset roughly one month to collect early signals before execution Day 90. This is still an early decision window, not a ranking or lead guarantee.

## Source of Truth and Update Rule

This file is the single programme tracker. Weekly specs are the implementation plan and evidence record.

When a weekly spec closes:

1. check every acceptance criterion against evidence;
2. update the weekly spec's `Status` and `Completion Record`;
3. update the calendar and checkpoint ledger in this file in the same change set;
4. record source path, live URL, publication/revision date, owner, commit/deploy, indexing state and next comparison date;
5. annotate material publication and measurement changes so later movement is not attributed from memory;
6. carry unfinished work with a named owner and date; never mark it complete silently; and
7. revise later specs only from documented capacity, query, lead or indexing evidence.

Commit, push, production deploy, Google/GA4/Netlify/directory changes, customer contact and publication of any customer identity require explicit authority during the relevant work.

## Prioritisation and Capacity Rules

- Maximum planned work in progress is two primary public assets per week.
- When capacity is threatened, finish the commercial service page before its companion article.
- Fix a broken conversion, indexing or trust dependency before starting optional content.
- Each URL owns one primary buyer intent. A brief must name target query cluster, audience stage, promise, proof, CTA, internal-link parents/children and measurement date.
- No article is commissioned merely to maintain volume. Every article must support a commercial page and answer a distinct buyer decision.
- One accountable owner and one approver are recorded per asset before drafting.
- A page is not counted as published until live, crawlable, internally linked, usable on mobile and connected to a working conversion path.

## Operating Rhythm

- **Monday:** read the spec, resolve carry-over, confirm owner/capacity and freeze the week's exact briefs.
- **Tuesday:** research primary sources and write the service page or highest-value asset first.
- **Wednesday:** implement, edit, add proof, internal links, metadata, CTA and the unique image where a blog post is planned.
- **Thursday:** editorial/technical review, repository-native validation and publication/deploy after explicit approval.
- **Friday:** verify live URL, form/CTA, sitemap, canonical, Search Console state and evidence; update both Completion Record and master tracker.

If approval or factual evidence is missing, Thursday produces a review-ready draft and Friday records the blocker. Nothing is rushed live to satisfy cadence.

- 1 About/Expertise trust page;

- 1 approved client case study or clearly labelled first-party engineering proof;
- 1 ungated Software Project Handover Checklist;
- privacy-safe lead measurement and service-specific conversion paths; and
- complete reciprocal internal links between commercial pages, content and proof.

The second proof asset and earned-authority work belong to the final 30 days because they require external permission and response time. They do not block the core production gate.

Blog articles follow the repository's DuniaOps workflow: original British English, primary-source verification, one buyer problem, natural service links, accurate front matter, a unique approved 1200x630 image and full generated-output validation. Drafts remain `draft: true` until publication is explicitly approved.

## Measurement Design

Use both programme windows and publication cohorts:

- **Programme view:** rolling 28 days versus previous 28 days at Day 30, 60 and 90.
- **Page cohort view:** compare each asset only after recording its publication date and at least 7-, 14- and 28-day observations.
- **Query-cluster view:** rescue, support, DevOps/Kubernetes, custom software and AI are measured separately.
- **Conversion view:** reconcile organic enquiries against Netlify Forms privately; GA4 remains directional under consent.
- **Change log:** record every material title, copy, URL, link, CTA and deploy date before interpreting movement.

Track:

- production completion and live/indexed state;
- impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by cluster/page;
- queries in positions 1–10, 11–30, 31–50 and 51+;
- organic engaged sessions;
- Netlify-verified qualified organic enquiries;
- privacy-safe service/landing-page attribution;
- relevant referring domains and citations; and
- genuine Google Business Profile/Clutch reviews.

Search Console data may arrive after a processing delay, and search improvements may take from days to months. Therefore Day 30 and Day 60 are leading-signal gates; the final complete-data check occurs after Day 90. References: [Google on timing of search improvements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates) and [Search Console data timing](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17010575?hl=en).

## Success Criteria

### Controllable delivery targets

- 100% of Day-60 core assets are live or explicitly blocked with owner/date;
- every published asset has working CTA, reciprocal links, metadata, canonical, sitemap and validation evidence;
- every material change has a publication annotation and 28-day comparison date; and
- no unapproved customer proof, unsupported claim, PII leakage, purchased link or incentivised review.

### Early outcome targets — directional, not guarantees

- at least 20 high-intent non-brand queries receiving impressions;
- at least 5 tracked queries in positions 1–30;
- 1–3 long-tail queries in positions 1–10;
- 5–10 relevant new referring domains or verified external citations;
- 3–5 genuine reviews across eligible surfaces; and
- at least one verified qualified organic enquiry.

## Reminder Behaviour

The active weekly heartbeat runs Monday and Friday at 09:00 Europe/London through the complete-data tail.

- Monday names the phase, current spec, exact primary publication/outcome, owners, carry-over and Monday–Friday checklist.
- Friday classifies each deliverable and acceptance criterion as `DONE`, `NOT DONE`, `BLOCKED` or `NOT VERIFIED`.
- Week 05 triggers Day-30 leading signals; Week 09 enforces the Day-60 core-production gate; Week 13 and the tail complete Day-90 measurement.
- From Week 10 onward, the reminder must reject quota-driven new articles and ask for the strongest evidence-led indexing, CTR, conversion, proof or authority action.
- The reminder is read-only. It may offer to start work but cannot edit, publish, deploy, contact people or mutate external accounts without a new explicit instruction.

## Day-90 Decision Framework

- Rankings and qualified leads improve: scale the winning cluster with stronger proof and adjacent buyer questions.
- Impressions improve but clicks do not: inspect intent, position and snippet fit before rewriting the page.
- Clicks improve but qualified leads do not: revise offer, proof, CTA and lead qualification.
- Pages are crawled but invisible: investigate usefulness, overlap, internal links and authority.
- Priority pages are not indexed: resolve technical/indexing causes before producing more content.
- Leads are low quality: narrow ICP, page promise, query focus and form routing.
- No cluster has a credible signal: stop broad production and run a focused offer/query reset.
