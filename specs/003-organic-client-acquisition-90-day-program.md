## Prioritisation and Capacity Rules

- Maximum planned work in progress is two primary public assets per week.
- When capacity is threatened, finish the commercial service page before its companion article.
- Fix a broken conversion, indexing or trust dependency before starting optional content.
- Each URL owns one primary buyer intent. A brief must name target query cluster, audience stage, promise, proof, CTA, internal-link parents/children and measurement date.
- No article is commissioned merely to maintain volume. Every article must support a commercial page and answer a distinct buyer decision.
- One accountable owner and one approver are recorded per asset before drafting.
- A page is not counted as published until live, crawlable, internally linked, usable on mobile and connected to a working conversion path.

## Measurement Design

Use both programme windows and publication cohorts:

- **Programme view:** rolling 28 days versus previous 28 days at Day 30, 60 and 90.
- **Page cohort view:** compare each asset only after recording its publication date and at least 7-, 14- and 28-day observations.
- **Query-cluster view:** rescue, support, DevOps/Kubernetes, custom software and AI are measured separately.
- **Conversion view:** reconcile organic enquiries against Netlify Forms privately; GA4 remains directional under consent.
- **Change log:** record every material title, copy, URL, link, CTA and deploy date before interpreting movement.

Track:

- production completion and live/indexed state;
- impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by cluster/page;
- queries in positions 1–10, 11–30, 31–50 and 51+;
- organic engaged sessions;
- Netlify-verified qualified organic enquiries;
- privacy-safe service/landing-page attribution;
- relevant referring domains and citations; and
- genuine Google Business Profile/Clutch reviews.

Search Console data may arrive after a processing delay, and search improvements may take from days to months. Therefore Day 30 and Day 60 are leading-signal gates; the final complete-data check occurs after Day 90. References: [Google on timing of search improvements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates) and [Search Console data timing](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17010575?hl=en).

## Reminder Behaviour

The active weekly heartbeat runs Monday and Friday at 09:00 Europe/London through the complete-data tail.

- Monday names the phase, current spec, exact primary publication/outcome, owners, carry-over and Monday–Friday checklist.
- Friday classifies each deliverable and acceptance criterion as `DONE`, `NOT DONE`, `BLOCKED` or `NOT VERIFIED`.
- Week 05 triggers Day-30 leading signals; Week 09 enforces the Day-60 core-production gate; Week 13 and the tail complete Day-90 measurement.
- From Week 10 onward, the reminder must reject quota-driven new articles and ask for the strongest evidence-led indexing, CTR, conversion, proof or authority action.
- The reminder is read-only. It may offer to start work but cannot edit, publish, deploy, contact people or mutate external accounts without a new explicit instruction.

## Day-90 Decision Framework

- Rankings and qualified leads improve: scale the winning cluster with stronger proof and adjacent buyer questions.
- Impressions improve but clicks do not: inspect intent, position and snippet fit before rewriting the page.
- Clicks improve but qualified leads do not: revise offer, proof, CTA and lead qualification.
- Pages are crawled but invisible: investigate usefulness, overlap, internal links and authority.
- Priority pages are not indexed: resolve technical/indexing causes before producing more content.
- Leads are low quality: narrow ICP, page promise, query focus and form routing.
- No cluster has a credible signal: stop broad production and run a focused offer/query reset.
