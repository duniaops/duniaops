### English version

A photo app handles memories people may never be able to replace. That makes privacy and user control concrete engineering decisions.

At DuniaOps, we’ve shared a case study from building Lumo, our own photo app for iPhone and iPad.

It explores three decisions behind the product:

- Photo analysis and indexing run on the device.
- Users can choose different photographs from those recommended.
- Removing photos requires explicit confirmation.

These decisions shape the interface, permissions, data flow and testing.

For teams planning a mobile product, the case offers practical questions about sensitive data, automated suggestions and actions with lasting consequences.
