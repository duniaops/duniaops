### Candidate 1 — Consolidating a fragmented enterprise application estate

- **Safe context:** users had to move between multiple distributed portals, while error visibility, work tracking and cross-application hand-offs were difficult.
- **Safe work narrative:** introduced an incremental micro-frontend approach and a common portal; modernised selected modules while retaining necessary legacy applications; then progressively centralised and separated backend capabilities to reduce coupling.
- **Safe qualitative outcome:** one user entry point and a more manageable path for continued modernisation. The solution was completed, released to production and handed over; the resulting improvements were accepted by the customer and demonstrated through performance metrics.
- **Week 02 use:** a concise proof block on About and Project Rescue, supporting the ability to inherit fragmented systems and modernise them in stages.
- **Publication boundary:** keep scale and metric values general, and omit identifying or contract-sensitive detail.

**Draft proof copy:**

> **Consolidating a fragmented enterprise application estate**
>
> In an earlier partner-led engagement, a distributed multi-portal estate was progressively brought behind a common user experience using a micro-frontend architecture. Selected modules were modernised without forcing a high-risk, big-bang replacement of the legacy estate, while backend capabilities were decomposed into more autonomous services with clearer boundaries and reduced coupling. The solution was completed, released to production and handed over to the customer, with the operational improvements accepted by stakeholders and demonstrated through performance measurement.

### Candidate 2 — Replacing spreadsheet-led booking allocation

- **Safe context:** a legacy CRM was combined with spreadsheets, email and phone calls to manage bookings and accommodation, leaving delays, exceptions and performance evidence difficult to record consistently.
- **Safe work narrative:** built a purpose-designed allocation system to centralise the workflow, make missed or delayed work more visible and support more consistent KPI evaluation.
- **Safe qualitative outcome:** the system was completed, released to production and handed over; the process improvements were accepted by the customer and demonstrated through performance metrics.
- **Week 02 use:** a concise About proof block. Retain the fuller narrative for the existing Booking & Allocation service and the future Custom Software page after factual review.
