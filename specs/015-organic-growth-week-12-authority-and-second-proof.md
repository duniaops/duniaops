**Status:** Planned
**Type:** Proof / Distribution / Authority
**Priority:** P1

## Outcome

Strengthen trust and discoverability with verifiable evidence and relevant third-party signals instead of mass directory or link activity.

- [ ] Select and write the second proof asset with an evidence/permission matrix.
- [ ] Link the proof from the most relevant service and buyer-content pages.

## Working Schedule

### Monday — proof and target selection

- Freeze the evidence-approved proof narrative.
- Select a small relevant target list; reject paid-link, bulk-directory and unrelated placements.

### Tuesday — write

- Write the proof page and personalised outreach/review/social messages.
- Separate verified metrics from estimates and confidential material.

### Wednesday — implement and connect

- Build the proof page, metadata, visuals and reciprocal internal links.
- Verify profile facts and destination URLs before any external action.

### Thursday — approve and publish

- Obtain final factual/client approval and run repository-native validation.
- Publish the proof and perform external mutations/messages only with explicit approval.

### Friday — close

- Verify the proof live and record outreach sent separately from links/reviews actually earned.
- Record citations, referring domains, profile actions, review state and future follow-ups.
- Update Completion Record and master tracker.

- [ ] Proof ownership, facts, permissions and commercial relevance are explicit.

- [ ] External actions are personalised, relevant and explicitly approved.
- [ ] `npm test`, link/mobile/desktop/accessibility and `git diff --check` pass.
- [ ] Live proof and authority-ledger evidence are recorded.

## Completion Record

Record proof path/URL, permission evidence location, validation, commit/deploy, approved external actions, outreach sent, links/citations/reviews earned, cohort date and outstanding responses.
