Built for software decisions that carry real operational weight

DuniaOps is an independent software and DevOps consultancy based in London. We work with founders, operations teams and technology leaders when a system needs to be understood properly, delivered responsibly or brought back under control.

Our working model is deliberately direct. The same senior engineering judgement used to shape the architecture stays close to implementation, production readiness and handover. That continuity matters when the work spans legacy applications, business-critical workflows, cloud infrastructure or several delivery partners.

We favour progressive modernisation over fashionable rewrites, evidence over confident guesswork, and technical choices that leave the client with clearer ownership than they started with.

Working principles

What stays true from assessment to handover

The technology can change. The standards around evidence, ownership and operational continuity should not.

01

Start from evidence

We map the real system, constraints and critical workflows before recommending a rewrite, migration or new feature programme.

02

Modernise in controlled stages

Where possible, valuable legacy behaviour remains available while new boundaries, modules and delivery practices are introduced progressively.

03

Design for client control

Repositories, infrastructure, documentation and operational knowledge are structured so the organisation is not trapped behind one supplier.
