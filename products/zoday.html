More help with Zoday →
Do I need to sign in?

No login is required to start. Zoday creates an anonymous app account, which you can choose to protect later.

What can I use without Plus?

Daily sign readings, the core Moon calendar, personal lunar-return dates, daily check-ins and your private journal do not require a subscription. Your existing journal records remain accessible.

Is my daily horoscope unique to me?

The daily horoscope is shared by zodiac sign. Your personal lunar-return dates are calculated separately from the birth details you provide.

Will I see patterns straight away?

No. Comparisons depend on enough actual journal entries. They describe your saved reflections; they do not establish that the Moon caused a feeling or outcome.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Zoday provides in-app JSON data export and permanent account deletion. You can also review and edit journal entries.

Are readings predictions or professional advice?

No. Horoscopes and traditional interpretations are entertainment. They are not predictions or medical, financial or legal advice. Astronomical data does not scientifically validate astrology.
