Meet the heroes

Eight space friends, each on its own asteroid

Every hero stands for a real size class, from a mouse-sized rock to a whale-sized one. Inside the app they blink, wiggle and wave.

Eight heroes · Forty chapters

Catch a visitor. Open a chapter. Keep the adventure.

Niko, Tavi, Barney, Kito, Pofi, Bobo, Ciko and Enoli each have a five-chapter story. Meet a hero on the Radar to unlock the next chapter; the Library keeps every story you find.

01

A living Radar

Today’s real near-Earth asteroids circle a familiar Earth. Spin, zoom and tap to meet each one.

02

Read and listen together

Hear the adventures in English or Turkish. Each fresh installation can choose one narrated chapter free; this is separate from the 10 free reading chapters. Download narration while connected, then listen to downloaded audio when your access allows it.

03

A Library that grows

Each hero’s first chapter and downloaded chapters can be read offline when you have access. New chapter and narration downloads, live sky data and purchases need a connection. Offline sample visitors are not today’s live sky.

Inside Rockimals

One sky. Many ways to explore.

Real science, gently told

The data stays real. The experience feels welcoming.

Meet an animal, compare its asteroid’s size with a familiar object and read a gentle space fact. Official asteroid details remain in the grown-up area behind the parent gate.

For grown-ups

Free at the core. Plus for more adventures.

Radar, animal facts and following are free, with 3 minutes of Earth Shield each day and one discovery mission per theme each day. Choose 10 free story chapters in total, with up to 3 new chapters per day. These free chapters stay yours to replay. Every plan, including Plus, discovers at most one new chapter per animal per day: finish the current chapter, then meet that friend on Radar on a later day.

While active, Rockimals Plus includes the published story library and available English/Turkish narration, unlimited Earth Shield, all 12 discovery missions, four more games and journal decorations. Daily chapter progression still applies.

FAQ

Good to know

Is Rockimals free?

Radar, animal facts and following are free, with 3 minutes of Earth Shield each day and one discovery mission per theme each day. Choose 10 free story chapters in total, with up to 3 new chapters per day. These free chapters stay yours to replay. Every plan, including Plus, discovers at most one new chapter per animal per day: finish the current chapter, then meet that friend on Radar on a later day.

Does it work offline?

Each hero’s first chapter and downloaded chapters can be read offline when you have access. New chapter and narration downloads, live sky data and purchases need a connection. Offline sample visitors are not today’s live sky.

Is the asteroid data real?

Yes. Rockimals uses NASA’s Near Earth Object Web Service and keeps the official designation available for grown-ups.
