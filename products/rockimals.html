FAQ

Good to know

Is Rockimals free?

Yes. The core Radar, animal facts, following and one new story a day are free. Rockimals Plus is optional.

Does it work offline?

Bundled stories and sample sky data keep the experience useful offline. Live visitors, new daily stories, purchases and reminders need a connection.

Is the asteroid data real?

Yes. Rockimals uses NASA’s Near Earth Object Web Service and keeps the official designation available for grown-ups.
