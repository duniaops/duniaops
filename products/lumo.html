Visit Lumo Support ↗
What can I review with Lumo?

On iPhone and iPad, start with a selection from your Photos library. On Mac, Lumo also works with folders, including common RAW and JPEG files supported by macOS.

Can I choose more than one photo from a similar group?

Yes. A recommendation gives you a starting point. Keep several frames or pick a different favourite after comparing them.

Where do removed photos go?

Photos library deletions go to Recently Deleted. For Mac folder reviews, files go to the system Trash by default. Lumo asks you to confirm removals.

Does Lumo post directly to social media?

You prepare the photos, order and caption in Lumo, then choose how to share or export through the options on your device. You approve the final post in the destination app.
