To unlock a Rockimals story, open **Radar**, tap a visiting animal friend and continue into its available chapter. Choose 10 free story chapters in total, with up to 3 new chapters per day. These free chapters stay yours to replay. Every plan, including Plus, discovers at most one new chapter per animal per day: finish the current chapter, then meet that friend on Radar on a later day.

## 1. Meet a hero on Radar

Each visitor on Radar is based on a real near-Earth object, but its animal friend and adventure are fiction. Tap a visitor to see its card, then choose the available story. If you are new to the app, our [first-session guide](/blog/how-to-play-rockimals) walks through that first tap.

The story catalog has eight heroes with five chapters each. These are fixed adventures, not new tales generated every morning. A visiting hero gives you a way into the next available part of that hero's adventure.

## 2. Read the chapter, then return another day

Enjoy the illustrated chapter at your own pace. For a later chapter of the **same hero**, finish the open chapter and meet that friend again on Radar on another day when it appears. Tapping the same friend again today opens the same chapter; it does not skip ahead. A specific hero is not guaranteed to visit every day.

Each hero’s first chapter and downloaded chapters can be read offline when you have access. New chapter and narration downloads, live sky data and purchases need a connection. Offline sample visitors are not today’s live sky.

## 3. Find chapters you have already opened

Open the **Story library** to return to an unlocked chapter. A library entry marked as locked is not a missing purchase: meet the relevant hero on Radar to open its next available chapter. If you have already used today's free new-story access, an unlocked chapter is still there to read again.

This makes a good short family routine: read one chapter, ask what the animal might do next, then find the same story in the library later. The animal and its adventure are imaginative; the object's designation and selected measurements come from space data.

## Free access and Rockimals Plus

While active, Rockimals Plus includes the published story library and available English/Turkish narration, unlimited Earth Shield, all 12 discovery missions, four more games and journal decorations. Daily chapter progression still applies.

Hear the adventures in English or Turkish. Each fresh installation can choose one narrated chapter free; this is separate from the 10 free reading chapters. Download narration while connected, then listen to downloaded audio when your access allows it.
