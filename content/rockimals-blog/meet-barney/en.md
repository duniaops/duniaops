Barney is the curious fox in Rockimals, with orange fur, a pale muzzle and a white-tipped tail. His five-chapter adventure begins close to home: a game with friends, a look at the sky and a wish to see somewhere new. You can meet him through the opening of his story without learning how the journey ends.

## A small clue starts a big question

At first, Barney is playing hide-and-seek in a garden with a rabbit and a turtle. A pair of ears peeking out from behind a tree gives you something to spot together. Before reading on, ask your child what they noticed in the picture.

Later, Barney and the rabbit watch a bright, magical rock cross the sky from a hill. For Barney, the interesting part is where it might be going. The picture gives a shared reading moment: look at the direction of his gaze, then imagine the question he might ask.

This is a fictional adventure. The glowing rock belongs to the story’s make-believe world; it is not a photograph of a real meteor or a way to travel in space.

## Curiosity needs a little preparation

Barney’s wish to explore comes with practical choices. In the opening chapter, he packs an apple and a small blanket in his yellow bag. A snack and something cosy make his big idea feel familiar.

Try a little packing game away from the screen. Each of you chooses two things for an imaginary journey and explains why. You do not need to match Barney’s choices, buy anything or finish the whole chapter in one sitting.

## Three questions for reading together

- **Notice:** Which detail helped you find a hidden friend?
- **Wonder:** What might Barney want to ask about the light in the sky?
- **Prepare:** What would you put in a small bag, and what would it help you do?

These are conversation starters, not a quiz in the app. Let your child point, draw or suggest another possibility. Keep predictions open until you read the next part together.

## Find the beginning in Rockimals

When Barney appears on **Radar**, meet him and check which chapter is available to you. To revisit a chapter you have already discovered, use the **Story library**. His appearance today and immediate access to every chapter are not guaranteed; our [story-unlocking guide](/blog/how-to-unlock-rockimals-stories) explains discovery and access.

For now, look for the moment that turns a familiar game into a new question. The rest of Barney’s journey is waiting in the illustrated story.
