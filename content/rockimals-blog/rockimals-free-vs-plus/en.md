**Yes. Rockimals is free to download and useful without a subscription.** You can explore Radar, read animal facts and follow visitors without paying. Rockimals Plus is an optional monthly or yearly subscription for families who want more of the published stories and games. You do not need Plus to meet a real space visitor.

## What can you do for free?

- Explore Radar, open visitor fact cards and follow animals.
- Choose **10 free reading chapters in total**, with up to **3 new chapters per day**. Chapters chosen through the free allowance remain available to reread.
- Play **3 minutes of Earth Shield each day** and complete **one discovery mission per theme per day**.
- On a fresh installation, choose **one narrated chapter free**. Narration is available in **English and Turkish**, and this audio allowance is separate from the 10 free reading chapters. It is not a daily free audio chapter.

The daily chapter limits are not a promise of newly written stories every day. Rockimals has a published story catalog, and its animal adventures are fiction alongside real visitor facts.

## What does Rockimals Plus add?

While the subscription is active, Plus includes access to the **published story library** and available English/Turkish narration, **unlimited Earth Shield**, **all 12 discovery missions**, **four additional games**, and journal decorations. These are additions to the free core, not a requirement to use Radar or read visitor facts.

Plus does **not** skip story progression. On every plan, you can discover at most **one new chapter per animal per day**: finish the current chapter, then meet that animal on Radar on a later eligible day. Free chapters already earned stay available to reread; do not assume subscription-only access remains after Plus ends.

## Price, renewal and a sensible first step

Plus is offered to grown-ups behind the parent gate. Check the price, billing period, any trial eligibility and renewal terms shown in your own store before confirming a purchase; they can differ by country and platform. An app uninstall does **not** cancel a subscription. If you subscribe through Apple or Google Play, manage or cancel it in that store's subscription settings.

Try Radar and a free chapter together first. If your family enjoys the free pace, you can stay there. Consider Plus only if broader access to the published library and extra games is useful to you.
