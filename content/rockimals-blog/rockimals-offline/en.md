**Yes, you can use parts of Rockimals without internet.** The app has sample visitors for Radar and each hero's first story chapter. Chapters already downloaded on your device can also be read offline when you have access to them. An offline sample sky is **not today's live asteroid data**.

## What works without a connection?

You can explore the sample visitors and revisit story chapters whose content is already on your device and whose access is still valid. A chapter you earned for free remains available to replay. Downloaded narration can play offline when it is available in your language and your access allows it. Downloading the story pictures and downloading narration are separate steps.

Offline mode does not bring in new live visitors or new chapter and narration files. Purchases and subscription changes also need a connection. If a chapter shows **Download**, it is not yet ready for a journey without internet; if you see a connection message, reconnect before trying again. Plus content remains subject to your applicable access, even if files were previously saved.

## A short checklist before you leave

1. While connected to Wi-Fi, open **Story library** and choose the chapters you want to read. Let any chapter download finish, then open it once to check that its pictures are ready.
2. If you plan to listen, prepare the available narration separately and check that the app says it is ready offline. Narration availability depends on the chapter, language and access.
3. Briefly turn on airplane mode and reopen a prepared chapter. This checks your *own device* before you travel. Turn the connection back on afterwards.
4. Tell your child that Radar may show sample visitors during the trip. When you reconnect, return to Radar for current visitor data.

![Editorial illustration inspired by Kito the tiger preparing a story for a trip; not a screenshot or proof that a chapter is downloaded](/assets/rockimals-blog/rockimals-offline/story-ready.jpg)

*A story scene for the journey. Check the chapter and narration on your own device before going offline.*

There is no promise that every game, newly discovered chapter or story language will work offline. If you change devices or reinstall the app, repeat the check rather than assuming downloaded files moved with you.

## Real sky and story world

Rockimals uses real near-Earth object data when it can load current visitors. Its animal friends and stories are fiction. Sample visitors help keep a playful sky on screen without a connection; they are not a forecast or a record of today's objects. Rockimals is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
