# DuniaOps AI search visibility assessment

**Assessment date:** 20 October 2026
**Audience:** Ibrahim Uylas and the DuniaOps organic-growth programme
**Decision:** Improve existing assets and complete the planned proof/checklist work. No new SEO subscription or additional blog series is justified by this assessment.

DuniaOps already has service-specific pages, buyer guides, clear engagement boundaries, disclosed engineering experience and company identity. The useful next investment is stronger evidence for those offers and completion of existing discovery work. A paid visibility tool would measure some of this more conveniently; this study does not establish that purchasing one would create enquiries.

## Scope and method

The assessment applies `duniaops-seo-opportunities` to five commercial services and ten fixed buyer questions. It combines public HTML checks, existing dated programme evidence, actual ChatGPT web-search answers and a small comparison of cited supplier pages. It is research only: no website content, crawler policy, account configuration or production deployment changed.

Each question was submitted once in a separate ChatGPT temporary chat, with the Web search tool selected. The prompt requested the UK market in English and did not name DuniaOps. The visible model selector said **Instant**; the underlying model/version was not exposed. The account was signed in. Temporary sessions reduce conversation carry-over, but location and all account-level personalisation were not independently controlled.

An answer that failed to load is **unavailable**, not a negative brand observation. Counts cover readable final answers and their visible links; collapsed source panels were not exhaustively expanded. The observation times are UTC; Europe/London was UTC+1 on the assessment date. This is a convenience sample, not market-wide share of voice, search volume, a ranking measurement or proof of causality.

## Technical accessibility

The public audit inspected five services, `/about` and seven supporting articles. All **13 returned HTTP 200**, had a single self-referencing canonical, appeared in the supplied sitemap, and had no observed meta or HTTP `noindex` directive. JSON-LD parsed in the sampled pages; semantic correctness and search-engine eligibility of each schema type were not established by that parser.

The live `robots.txt` returned:

```text
User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://www.duniaops.com/sitemap.xml
```

The sitemap contained 31 URLs. These are HTML observations using `DuniaOpsSEOAudit/1.0`, not evidence of an actual AI crawler visit or a complete CDN/WAF audit. The checks found no reason to change robots policy merely for this study.

Evidence: [service and About audit](evidence/ai-visibility-2026-10-20/services-audit.json), captured 07:01:34 UTC, and [article audit](evidence/ai-visibility-2026-10-20/articles-audit.json), captured 07:03:51 UTC.

OpenAI identifies OAI-SearchBot as its search crawler and separates it from GPTBot training controls. Search access does not require granting new training permission. If future crawler access is disputed, inspect real request logs against the published search-bot IP ranges; a request with a copied user-agent is insufficient. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)

Google requires a page to be indexed and eligible for a snippet to be a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode. It does not require a special AI file or schema. Consequently, `llms.txt` and additional FAQ markup are not priorities here. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

Three cited pages were opened directly to compare their published content:

This is a more specific evidence trail than our anonymous summary; the supplier's outcome claims were not independently audited.
- [Probase support](https://www.probase.co.uk/what-we-build/software-support/) states its supported Microsoft technology set and takeover sequence plainly. Our support page explains the sequence well but leaves technology fit to the review.
- [Strataform services](https://www.strataform.uk/services) explicitly addresses small teams/startups and names deliverables such as Terraform modules, runbooks and SLO dashboards. Our DevOps page already lists tools and Health Check outputs; a sample output could make that offer easier to evaluate.

These differences suggest experiments. They do not establish why ChatGPT selected a supplier or justify copying its pricing, promises or wording.

## Business measurement

Keep three separate measures:

1. **Observed answers:** readable answers mentioning DuniaOps and readable answers visibly linking to a DuniaOps URL, each with the eligible-answer denominator.
2. **Visits:** GA4 source/referrer and landing-page observations where consent and referrer data permit. Record direct/unknown traffic as unknown; do not reconstruct attribution from a citation.
3. **Qualified enquiries:** the existing private qualification process and measurement ledger. A form event, test submission, AI citation or visit is not automatically a qualified lead.
