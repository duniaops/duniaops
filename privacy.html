Privacy notice

How we use and protect information you share with DuniaOps.

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Who we are

DuniaOps Solution and Consulting Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales under company number 11173416. For questions about this notice or your personal information, email info@duniaops.com.

Information we collect

When you send a project enquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, work email, company, service interest and project description. If you contact us directly, we also receive the contents of that correspondence.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to understand your request, respond, prepare proposals, manage a potential or active business relationship, and protect the security of our services. We do not sell personal information.

Analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use this website. It may collect pages viewed, approximate location, referral source, and technical information about your browser and device. We do not send the contents of project enquiries to Google Analytics.

Analytics is disabled until you select “Accept optional cookies”. You can reject optional cookies without affecting the website and change your choice later using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Your choice is stored in your browser so we can remember it.

Service providers

This website is hosted by Netlify, which processes website requests and form submissions on our behalf. The site requests the Inter typeface from Google Fonts and, only after consent, uses Google Analytics for website measurement. These providers may process technical information such as IP address and browser details to deliver their services.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and business correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. Contact us at info@duniaops.com to make a request.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect enquiry information. Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom; where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website or processing changes. The latest version will always be published on this page with its effective date.
