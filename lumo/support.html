Get your photos into Lumo

Why can’t I see my whole photo library?

Check Lumo’s Photos permissions in your device’s Settings. If you granted access to selected photos on iPhone or iPad, Lumo can review only that selection. You can change what Lumo can see at any time.

How do I review a Mac folder?

Open the folder in Lumo. If the folder has moved, or it lives on a disconnected drive, reconnect the drive and let Lumo reopen the folder before resuming.

Review and free up space

Can I change which similar photos are kept?

Yes. Similar groups are suggestions. Open a group to compare frames and keep any number of them before you confirm the cleanup.

Where can I see which videos take up the most space?

Open the large-video review to compare file sizes and durations. Review the clips you choose and confirm changes separately from your photo selection.

Can I recover a removed photo?

In Apple Photos, look in Recently Deleted, where photos normally remain for about 30 days. For Mac folders, look in the system Trash if you used the default deletion setting. Permanent deletion and emptying Trash remove those recovery options.

Find, frame and share

Why isn’t my photo appearing in Search?

Check that you’re searching the right library selection or folder and that preparation has finished for those photos. Try a subject, words that are visible in the scene, or the filename. Optional Apple place lookup can improve place-name matches.

What happens to the original when I share?

Crop and frame changes are kept separately from the original. Review your draft and choose how to share or export your prepared copies.

Free access and purchases

What does the free experience include?

The free experience covers successful culling of 100 distinct photos and one eligible multi-photo sharing suggestion. Re-reviewing counted photos uses no extra allowance. There is no time limit or automatic paid subscription.

How do I restore Lumo Pro?

On iPhone or iPad, use Restore Purchases in Lumo with the Apple Account that acquired Pro. Monthly, annual and lifetime options grant access to the same Pro tools.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Apple handles payments and renewals. You can cancel via your Apple Account or request a refund from Apple. If you buy lifetime access while subscribed, manage your existing renewal separately.
