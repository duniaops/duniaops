A little help.
More room for memories.

Questions about Lumo? Find help with photo access, reviewing your gallery, and purchases below.

Talk to us

Email info@duniaops.com. Tell us your device, operating system, Lumo version, and what happened. You do not need to send personal photos or payment details.

Photo access

Lumo needs access to the photos you want to review. If your library looks incomplete, check Lumo’s photo permissions in your device’s Settings. You can choose which photos the app can access and change that choice later.

Review before removing

Lumo groups similar photos and suggests which ones to keep. You stay in control: review the selection before confirming a deletion. Photos removed through the Photos library go to Recently Deleted, where Apple normally keeps them for about 30 days.

Search and privacy

Photo analysis, recognized text, labels, and the search index stay on your device. Optional Apple place lookup is explained before you choose whether to enable it. Read the Lumo Privacy Policy for details.

The free experience

Try successful culling of 100 distinct photos in total and one eligible multi-photo sharing suggestion. Reviewing previously counted photos does not use more allowance. There is no time limit and no automatic paid subscription.

Purchases and restoration

Lumo Pro is available through monthly or annual subscriptions, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices and billing periods are shown before purchase. To restore access, use Lumo’s Restore Purchases option with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Manage a subscription or request a refund

Apple manages payments and subscriptions. You can cancel a subscription through your Apple Account or request a refund from Apple. A lifetime purchase does not automatically cancel an existing subscription.
