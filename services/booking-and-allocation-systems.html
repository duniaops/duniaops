Booking & Resource Allocation Platforms

Custom booking, scheduling and allocation systems that encode how your operation actually works — availability, conflicts, capacity, pricing, staff and assets — instead of forcing you into someone else's template.

When off-the-shelf stops fitting, we build what does

Most businesses start with a generic booking tool — and outgrow it the day their rules stop fitting the template. Double bookings that the tool can't prevent. Resources that depend on other resources. Pricing that changes by season, group size or member status. Staff who need rostering alongside rooms, vehicles or equipment. Spreadsheets creep back in, and the "system" becomes a person who knows how everything really works.

DuniaOps builds booking and allocation platforms around your exact operation. Your availability rules, your resources, your pricing, your brand — with no per-booking fees and full ownership of your customer data. Because we build with AI-accelerated workflows, a custom platform is now within reach of operations that could never have justified one before.

Availability & conflict engine

Real-time availability with your rules: capacities, dependencies, buffers, lead times and double-booking prevention.

Online booking & payments

A branded booking flow your customers use on any device — with payments, deposits, refunds and cancellation policies via Stripe.

Automated communications

Confirmations, reminders and follow-ups by email and SMS — cutting no-shows and manual admin.

Staff & asset allocation

Rostering and allocation of people, rooms, vehicles or equipment alongside bookings — one system, one truth.

Admin dashboard & reporting

A control panel for your team: manage bookings, override rules, track utilisation, export reports.

Integrations

Two-way calendar sync (Google/Outlook), CRM, accounting and marketing tools — your booking data where you need it.

FAQ

Booking platforms, honestly answered

Why custom instead of off-the-shelf booking software?

Off-the-shelf works until your rules don't fit its model — complex resources, multi-step allocations, custom pricing, industry-specific workflows. Custom encodes your exact rules, removes per-booking fees, and owns your data. With AI-accelerated development it's affordable for far smaller operations than before.

Can it take online payments?

Yes — payments, deposits, refunds and cancellation policies, typically via Stripe, plus invoicing flows for B2B bookings.

Does it integrate with our calendars and tools?

Yes — two-way Google/Outlook calendar sync, plus CRM, accounting, email/SMS and reporting integrations.

How long does it take to build?

A focused first version — availability, bookings, payments, reminders, admin dashboard — typically goes live in a few weeks. Rostering, complex allocation logic and further integrations follow in short iterations.
