FAQ

Mobile app development, plainly answered

Do you build native or cross-platform apps?

We normally recommend a modern cross-platform approach for business apps because it reduces duplicated work while retaining platform-specific behavior. If a product genuinely needs separate native implementations, we will say so during discovery.

Can the app connect to our existing systems?

Yes. We can integrate with existing APIs, identity providers, payment services, booking engines and internal platforms, or build the missing backend services where required.

Who owns the store accounts and code?

You do. The app is submitted through accounts your organisation controls, and the source code and supporting infrastructure are delivered to your repositories.

What happens after launch?

We can provide monitoring, OS compatibility updates, release support and product improvements, or hand over the documented system to your own team.
