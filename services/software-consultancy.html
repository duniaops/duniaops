FAQ

Software consultancy, honestly answered

Can you review a system without taking over delivery?

Yes. A review can be a standalone engagement with findings, priorities and a handover session. There is no obligation to use DuniaOps for implementation.

Will you work with our existing engineering team?

Yes. We can work alongside internal engineers and existing suppliers, focusing on the decisions or delivery gaps where senior support has the most value.

What does a review produce?

The exact output depends on the question, but normally includes evidence, key risks, recommended actions, sequencing and decisions that need leadership input.

Can you help recover a stalled project?

Yes. We first stabilise the situation and establish what is working, what is not, and what constraints are real. From there we propose a phased recovery plan rather than promising an instant rewrite.
